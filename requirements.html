Dataset



Here, we offer a comprehensive training database of ground-truth images meticulously curated to encompass a diverse array of features and dynamic ranges, meticulously crafted to be free from any noise or artifact structure. These images serve as invaluable resources for the robust training of deep-learning models.
Our database is constructed from a meticulous selection of ground-truth images sourced from real low-dynamic range astronomical and medical imaging sources. Radio astronomy images are meticulously gathered from esteemed repositories such as the National Radio Astronomy Observatory (NRAO) Archives, as well as prominent surveys like the LOFAR HBA Virgo cluster survey (Edler et al., 2023) and the LoTSS-DR2 survey (Shimwell et al., 2022). Optical astronomy images are meticulously curated from the prestigious National Optical-Infrared Astronomy Research Laboratory. In the realm of medical imaging, our selection process is equally rigorous, with images carefully chosen from the esteemed NYU fastMRI Initiative Database (Zbontar et al., 2018; Knoll et al., 2020).
Ground-truth images of size 512x512 are generated using the pre-processing procedure proposed in Terris et al., 2023. More precisely, various operations including concatenation, rotation, translation, and edge smoothing, are applied specifically to the medical images to deconstruct their anatomical features. Denoising is applied to all images, of both medical and astronomical origins, to eliminate artifacts and noise, using a denoising DNN (zhang et al.,2023) in combination with soft-thresholding operations. Additionally, a pixel-wise exponentiation transform can be applied to the curated ground-truth images to emulate the characteristic high dynamic range of radio images (Terris et al., 2022).
The validation dataset comprises images of giant radio galaxies (e.g., Messier 87; third column), and radio galaxy clusters (e.g., Abell746; fourth column)
Test datasets were created using four real radio images: the giant radio galaxies 3C353 (NRAO Archives) and Messier106 (Shimwell et al., 2022), and the radio galaxy clusters Abell2034 and PSZ2G165.68+44.01 (Botteon et al., 2022). From each radio image, 50 ground-truth images of size 512x512 with varying dynamic range were generated following the pre-processing procedure used for the training datasets. Different Fourier sampling patterns were used to simulate the RI data associated with the ground-truth images. The resulting test dataset comprises 200 inverse problems.
The comprehensive details about the provided dataset and the VLA sampling pattern can be found in the paper: The R2D2 deep neural network series paradigm for fast precision imaging in radio astronomy.
